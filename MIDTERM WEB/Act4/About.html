Our History

Founded in 1892, Liverpool Football Club is one of the most successful and historic clubs in English football. Based in Merseyside, the club competes in the Premier League and plays its home matches at the iconic Anfield stadium.

With a rich history of domestic and European success, the Reds have a passionate fanbase all around the world. The famous atmosphere of the Kop is known globally.
